Good questions

Before you
tee off

Don’t see yours here? Put it in your order request and we’ll get back to you.

What kind of artwork should I send?

The sharper the better. A high-resolution PNG, JPG or PDF works well, and a vector file (AI, EPS, SVG or PDF) is best if you have one. A clear photo of a printed logo is fine to get started.

I don’t have a logo. Can you still make them?

Yes. Names, dates, short messages and simple designs all work great. Tell us your idea and we’ll help put it together.

Will I see my design before it’s printed?

Yes. We set up your design on the ball and send it to you to approve before anything gets printed.

How many do I have to order?

Tell us how many you need, from a gift for one golfer to a whole tournament field, and we’ll let you know what’s possible and send a quote.

How fast can I get them?

Fast turnaround is one of our promises. Put your need-by date in your request and we’ll confirm we can hit it before you commit.

Can I choose the ball color?

White is the classic, and bright colors like orange make a design pop. Pick a color in your request and we’ll let you know what’s available.

Will the print hold up on the course?

Our printing is made to stay bright and long-lasting, round after round.
